Essay 3: Describe a time you meaningfully contributed to someone else’s sense of inclusion in your professional or personal community. (300 words)

Essay 3 Response: 
Sergeant Lauren Burd had outrun half the men on our team. She’d earned her place, but on race day, her spot was in jeopardy, not because of performance, but because she was a woman.

Every year, the 82nd Airborne Division celebrates its history with “All-American Week,” a series of unit competitions. One event is the All-American 10-Miler, and my commander chose me to lead our unit’s team in the cross-country race. On race morning, when we realized we had to cut several runners to meet event limits, my assistant immediately suggested cutting the only woman on our team.

My assistant felt an intense pressure to win, reminding me that our commander had stressed the importance of winning this high-profile event. Despite proving herself, her potential was being challenged by a systemic culture that underestimates women every day.  I remembered all the times I had seen these stigmas hinder women while at West Point, but I also remembered when women outperformed those short-sighted expectations at Ranger School. Reflecting on these experiences, I realized that while I had a duty to listen to those I led, I had a far more important obligation to create an environment where potential could be nurtured, not stifled. 

Without thinking of the potential consequences, I stopped the conversation and handed Lauren a bib. 

While we didn’t win, Lauren placed first among all female competitors, earning her a spot representing our unit at the 80th anniversary of D-Day. After the race, Lauren thanked me: “Sir, I heard your conversation this morning and almost left, but you believed in me, so I stayed.”

That day, I had almost let a cultural bias hinder Lauren’s ability to succeed. I’m glad that in a difficult moment, I was able to lead in a way that empowered Lauren. Inspired by that experience, at Tuck, I’ll continue to create an inclusive environment where others can thrive, making sure that every teammate, just like Lauren, has a place where they can reach their full potential.
