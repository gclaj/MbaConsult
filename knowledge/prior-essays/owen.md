Essay 2: We are genuinely interested in getting to know you and recognize that your identity extends beyond the confines of this application's structured questions. Please highlight something about yourself that isn't already captured in the application. This highlight statement is an opportunity for you to provide context and depth to something that is important or meaningful to you (200 word limit)

Essay 2 Response: 
I am a vagabond. Within five years, I moved from inner-city Baltimore to suburban Las Vegas, to a small town in Vermont. People often assume that constant upheaval made childhood difficult, but it shaped me into someone adaptive, resilient, and deeply attuned to culture.

I realized the strength of those traits when I was asked to lead 35 volunteers in Douala, Cameroon. Drawn from five continents, our team was passionate but misaligned. Leveraging my own experience bridging cultures, I rebuilt cohesion by coaching peers, establishing shared systems, and strengthening partnerships.

That instinct resurfaced when I failed the first phase of Ranger School. Instead of retreating inward, I organized late-night study sessions and helped reignite morale. When 87% of our group ultimately passed, my pride came not from my own success, but from the team culture we built together.

These experiences have shaped me into someone who finds genuine joy in coaching others and building systems that unlock collective potential. At Owen, I will bring that passion to OOAC, the Armed Forces Club, and the classroom, helping foster a community among my peers where students not only achieve individually, but rise together.
